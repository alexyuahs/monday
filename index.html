Monday

A personal tool that sends its owner's own study notes to the owner's own Google Drive.

What Monday does

Monday is a small personal desktop tool used by one person, its developer. It exports notes that the owner has written in Obsidian into documents and uploads them into a single folder named Monday-Sync in the owner's own Google Drive. From there the owner can add the documents as sources in NotebookLM to study with.

It is not a public service, has no accounts of its own, and is not offered to other users.

How it uses Google data

  • Monday signs in with Google OAuth and requests only one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This lets the app see and change only the files it has created itself. It cannot read any other file in Google Drive.
  • It creates the folder Monday-Sync and Google Docs inside it, and updates those documents when the owner's notes change.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. Data travels only between the owner's computer and Google.
